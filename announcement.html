Two new short courses are joining our fall lineup

MP MdR Palacios · Generation Data
August 12, 2026 · 4 min read

I'm excited to share that two of our longtime volunteer trainers, Neal Hussein and Nancy Stalkner, are bringing back two of our most requested short courses this September — Cybersecurity Basics and Spreadsheet Power. Both are free, both are online, and both are built specifically for organizers, canvassers, and nonprofit teams who work with sensitive data every day.

Since we relaunched our short-course series this summer, the question I've heard most often from partner organizations is some version of: "This is great, but do you have anything on keeping our data and accounts safe?" and "Can someone just teach my team Excel already?" So this fall, we're saying yes to both.

Neal and Nancy have each taught these sessions for our staff and partner orgs before, and the materials below build on training they've already run — updated for this cohort and the current moment. Here's what's coming, when it's happening, and how to grab a seat.

Course 1

Cybersecurity Basics

2 hours · one session

Taught by Neal Hussein, National Government Affairs Data Manager at CWA

Neal built this primer for people who work in labor and politics and need to be, in his words, "way more careful than you currently are." It's a plain-language walkthrough of why we should care about digital security, who the realistic attackers are, and what to actually do about it — no technical background required.

What we'll cover

  • Why this matters for organizers
  • Who the attackers really are
  • Common attack types
  • Protecting your devices
  • Protecting your communications
  • Spotting a compromised account
  • What to do if you've been hit
Thursday, Sept 10, 2026 7:00 – 9:00 PM ET · Zoom

Course 2

Spreadsheet Power

4 hours · two sessions

Taught by Nancy Stalkner

Nancy's spreadsheet training is the one people keep asking us to bring back — a hands-on, no-fluff walk through the Excel skills that actually come up in organizing and campaign work: cleaning messy contact lists, building lookups between datasets, and turning raw numbers into reporting your team can act on. Split across two evenings so there's time to practice between sessions.

What we'll cover

  • Formulas & anchoring references
  • Working with dates
  • SUM, AVERAGE, SUMIF, COUNTIF
  • CONCATENATE & TRIM
  • Text to Columns
  • LEFT / RIGHT / MID
  • Filters & VLOOKUP
  • Pivot tables
  • IF statements & de-duplication
  • Conditional formatting
Tuesday, Sept 22, 2026 6:00 – 8:00 PM ET · Zoom · Session 1
Thursday, Sept 24, 2026 6:00 – 8:00 PM ET · Zoom · Session 2
