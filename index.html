Course 1

Spreadsheet Power for Progressive Teams

Nancy Stalkner•6 hours•Trending use case

This page positions the spreadsheet class as a practical training for organizers, nonprofit staff, and operations teams who need to turn messy lists into reliable reporting, outreach tracking, and leadership-ready summaries.

What participants practice

  • Cleanup workflows for contact lists, event attendance data, and outreach universes.
  • Hands-on formulas, filters, lookups, summaries, and quick visual reporting.
  • A progressive use case lab built around campaign, civic engagement, or community follow-up data.

Included takeaways

  • Outreach universe cleanup tracker.
  • Weekly organizer metrics dashboard.

Course 2

Cybersecurity for Progressive and Nonprofit Professionals

Neal Hussein•6 hours•Operations and risk

This landing page frames the course as a practical security offering for nonprofit professionals and politically sensitive teams, with room to address surveillance concerns, ICE-related risk contexts, and safer staff workflows at a level that remains useful for non-technical audiences.

What participants practice

  • Account protection basics such as multifactor authentication, password managers, and recovery plans.
  • Field risk topics including phishing, device safety, metadata awareness, and location-sharing habits.
  • Operational practices for shared drives, offboarding, and incident response in small organizations.

Included takeaways

  • Nonprofit cybersecurity self-audit checklist.
  • Incident response and account lockout escalation template.

Course 3

Introducción en Español a Datos Progresistas

Organizadores hispanohablantes•6 horas•Defender el voto

Esta página presenta un curso corto para organizadores hispanohablantes que necesitan usar datos para proteger acceso al voto, documentar problemas, coordinar seguimiento y trabajar con confianza en contextos donde idioma, seguridad y participación cívica se cruzan.

Lo que practican los participantes

  • Fundamentos de listas, calidad de datos y seguimiento para trabajo comunitario y electoral.
  • Uso práctico de hojas de cálculo y herramientas de organización bilingüe.
  • Escenarios de apoyo en las urnas, documentación de incidencias y rutas de escalamiento.

Plantillas incluidas

  • Plantilla de seguimiento de incidencias en las urnas.
  • Plantilla bilingüe de contacto y seguimiento.
